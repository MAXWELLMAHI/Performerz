If the student is under the age of eighteen (18) years, these terms must be agreed to and executed by a parent or legally recognized adult guardian.

02 // ADMISSIONS & TRIALS

Admissions, Eligibility & Demo Trials

Admission to specific training levels (Foundational, Intermediate, Advanced Company) is subject to assessment by our certified faculty directors.

  • Trial & Demo Class Bookings: Demo class slots booked via our website or WhatsApp are subject to schedule availability. The Academy reserves the right to reschedule trial sessions with advance notice.
  • Batch Allocation: Placement in specialized batches (e.g. Acrobatics, Aerial Poise, Contemporary Release) is determined by age, physiological readiness, and technical proficiency to ensure a safe learning environment.
  • Right of Refusal: The Academy reserves the right to decline or suspend admission to any individual whose conduct is disruptive, unsafe, or contrary to the ethos of the atelier.
03 // PHYSICAL SAFETY & WAIVER

Athletic Demands & Physical Risk Waiver

Performing arts, dance, gymnastics, tumbling, and aerial silks involve rigorous cardiovascular exertion, flexibility conditioning, elevation, and acrobatic maneuvering.

⚠️ Express Assumption of Physical Risk

You acknowledge that participation in dance and acrobatics carries inherent physical risks (including muscle strains, sprains, falls, or unforeseen injuries). You voluntarily assume all risks associated with participation and affirm that you (or your enrolled minor) are in sound physical health with no undisclosed medical conditions restricting intense physical activity.

  • Students must immediately inform instructors of any discomfort, dizziness, pain, or existing injury prior to or during warm-ups and choreography drills.
  • Aerial apparatus (silks, hoops) and tumbling crash mats must only be utilized under the direct active supervision of a certified Performerz instructor.

Medical Emergency Authorization

In the rare event of an acute medical emergency occurring on studio premises where the emergency contact cannot be immediately reached, you authorize The Performerz Academy faculty and staff to secure necessary emergency medical assistance, first aid, or hospital transport. Any medical expenses incurred from external emergency care providers remain the financial responsibility of the student or parent/guardian.

05 // FINANCIAL SCHEDULES

Tuition Fees, Payment Terms & Refund Policy

Tuition fees support the continuous maintenance of state-of-the-art sprung dance floors, certified aerial rigging, high-fidelity acoustics, and master-level pedagogical instruction.

  • Advance Tuition: Term and monthly fees must be paid in full prior to the start of each training cycle.
  • Strict No-Refund Policy: Tuition fees paid are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable once the batch has commenced. No refunds or fee prorations are provided for student absences, voluntary dropouts, or personal scheduling conflicts.
  • Medical Freeze Option: In cases of medically certified severe injuries preventing training for more than four (4) consecutive weeks, a formal request accompanied by certified physician documentation may be evaluated for a tuition credit freeze.
06 // ATELIER CODE

Studio Etiquette & Attire Code

To maintain the highest standards of safety, discipline, and artistic focus, all students agree to adhere to the Atelier Etiquette Code:

  • Punctuality: Dancers must arrive at least 10 minutes prior to scheduled batch times. Proper warm-ups are mandatory to prevent severe muscle strain; late arrivals exceeding 15 minutes may be restricted from strenuous exercises.
  • Studio Attire: Form-fitting dancewear (leotards, leggings, athletic tights). Baggy clothing, loose jewelry, wristwatches, and sharp hairpins are strictly prohibited on aerial apparatus and tumbling mats.
  • Footwear: Specialized dance shoes (contemporary socks, ballet slippers, or bare feet depending on syllabus). Outdoor footwear is never permitted on sprung studio dance floors.
  • Respect & Inclusivity: Zero tolerance for discrimination, bullying, harassment, or disrespectful behavior toward fellow performers, instructors, or staff.
07 // ATTENDANCE & MAKE-UPS

Attendance & Make-up Class Guidelines

Consistent attendance is vital for group choreography cohesion and safe progressive conditioning:

  • Dancers should provide prior notice via WhatsApp/Email if they will miss a scheduled class.
  • Make-up classes are offered subject to batch availability and instructor approval within the current active billing cycle. Unused make-up sessions do not carry forward into subsequent terms.
  • The Academy reserves the right to adjust batch schedules during public festivals, national holidays, or special masterclass events.
