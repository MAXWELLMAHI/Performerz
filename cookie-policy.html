01 // ESSENTIAL CONCEPTS

What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text data files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. Local Storage and Session Storage are modern HTML5 web technologies that allow websites to securely remember user interface preferences, preloader calibration status, and audio toggle states without sending data over every server request.

At The Performerz Academy, we use these technologies to provide you with a fluid, responsive, and visually stunning digital experience while respecting your privacy rights.

02 // ATELIER PURPOSE

Why We Use Tracking & Storage Technologies

Our digital platform utilizes cookies and local storage for specific functional purposes:

  • Site Performance & Preloading: Calibrating asset loaders so the website loads smoothly without jarring visual shifts.
  • Video Playback Synchronization: Remembering video mute/unmute preferences for our background athletic choreographies.
  • User Consent Records: Remembering whether you have acknowledged our Cookie Consent Banner so you aren't repeatedly prompted on every page load.
  • Aggregated Telemetry: Measuring high-level anonymous visits and page interaction times to improve our class syllabus presentations.
03 // CATEGORIZATION

Cookie Classifications

We categorize all cookies and storage items into four clear tiers:

1. Strictly Necessary / Essential Always Active

Required for core security, network navigation, preloader calibration, and basic responsive functionality. Without these, the atelier website cannot operate properly.

2. Performance & Video Buffering

Assists in video frame caching, responsive bandwidth adaptation for 4K/HD dance reels, and measuring asset download speeds.

3. Analytics & Experience Optimization

Provides anonymous, aggregated traffic statistics such as the most viewed dance disciplines and navigation flows, helping us refine our curriculum guides.

4. Social Media & Media Embeds

Enables embedded Instagram reel playback and direct WhatsApp API messaging linkage.

06 // THIRD PARTIES

Third-Party Media Cookies

Some sections of our site feature embedded content from third-party services like Instagram (Meta) and WhatsApp. When you interact with embedded reels or click to open WhatsApp, these third parties may set cookies to track interaction or maintain login sessions on their respective domains.

We do not have direct control over third-party cookie algorithms. We encourage you to review Meta's Cookie Policy for detailed terms.

07 // BROWSER CONTROLS

Managing Cookies in Your Web Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie settings, reject specific tracking cookies, or clear your browsing cache:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari (macOS & iOS): Preferences / Settings → Privacy → Prevent Cross-Site Tracking & Block All Cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Note: Disabling all cookies may affect video playback smoothness and site appearance.
