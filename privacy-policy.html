01 // PREAMBLE

Overview & Atelier Scope

Welcome to The Performerz Academy ("we," "our," "us," or "the Atelier"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, handle, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website (performerz.in / performerz.vercel.app), register for trial demo classes, enroll in studio programs (Gymnastics, Contemporary, Aerial Silks, Tumbling), or communicate with our admissions team.

By accessing our services or submitting details via our contact forms, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. We adhere strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India) and apply global privacy principles (including GDPR standards) for our international visiting dancers.

02 // DATA ACQUISITION

Personal Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our website or register at our studio:

  • Identification & Contact Data: Full name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp contact number, and residential city.
  • Student Profile & Enrollment Data: Age group, date of birth, previous dance/athletic experience, preferred discipline (e.g. Acrobatics, Aerial, Contemporary), and class batch timings.
  • Parent/Guardian Details: For minors under 18 years of age, emergency contact person, parent/guardian name, and emergency phone line.
  • Physical Readiness & Medical Disclosures: Voluntary disclosures of prior musculoskeletal injuries, heart conditions, or physical accommodations necessary to ensure safe physical instruction.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, screen resolution, referrers, and user interactions with our video showcases and interactive curricula.
03 // PROCESSING RATIONALE

Purpose & Use of Information

Your data is collected strictly for legitimate atelier operations and student safety:

  • Demo Class Scheduling: Coordinating trial slots, confirming studio studio space, and matching performers to suitable instructor levels.
  • Admissions & Enrollment: Managing active student rosters, attendance tracking, and syllabus progression.
  • Safety & Emergency Protocols: Contacting parents or emergency personnel in the rare event of athletic strain or injury during intense acrobatic training.
  • Atelier Updates & Showcases: Sending critical schedule changes, recital announcements, masterclass invites, and fee receipt confirmations.
  • Website Performance Optimization: Ensuring fast 60fps video streaming, interactive module responsiveness, and high audio-visual fidelity.
🔒 No Sale of Personal Data

The Performerz Academy does not sell, rent, or trade student or visitor personal data to any third-party marketing brokers. Your data is used exclusively to facilitate your training and communication with our academy.

04 // YOUTH PROTECTION

Minor & Child Performer Safety

As an academy providing specialized dance, tumbling, and aerial education to young artists, safeguarding minors is of utmost priority.

  • All demo class requests and enrollments for students under the age of 18 must be authorized and submitted by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Direct communication regarding schedules, fees, and performance updates for minor students is routed directly to the verified parent/guardian WhatsApp number or email address.
  • Parents may inspect, update, or request the deletion of their child's records at any time by contacting the Atelier administration.
05 // VISUAL RELEASES

Photo & Video Media Consent

Dance, choreography, and acrobatics are inherently visual arts. During routine studio sessions, masterclasses, and stage showcases, photos and video reels may be recorded for pedagogical evaluation and academy archival purposes.

Upon enrollment, students or their guardians are presented with an optional media release option. If you prefer yourself or your child not to appear in public promotional video reels (e.g. Instagram showcases or website header reels), you can notify us in writing during registration, and we will place an exclusion flag on your profile.

08 // SECURITY & STORAGE

Data Security & Retention

We implement robust physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to preserve the confidentiality and integrity of your information:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 Encryption: All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted in transit.
  • Restricted Staff Access: Only certified administrative directors and lead instructors have access to student emergency contact records.
  • Retention Periods: Inactive student records are purged or anonymized within 24 months of program completion unless retention is required by taxation or legal mandates.
09 // YOUR RIGHTS

Your Data Rights & Access Controls

Under applicable data protection legislation, you hold the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a summary copy of all personal records held by the academy.
  • Right of Correction: Update outdated phone numbers, email addresses, or health disclosures.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your records upon cessation of enrollment.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for promotional messaging or photography at any point.
